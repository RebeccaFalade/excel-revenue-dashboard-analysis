# Excel Revenue Dashboard Analysis
**Overall Summary**
This dashboard visualizes revenue (Sum of line total) and quantity sold (sum of quantity) across four regions (East, North, South, West) and multiple product categories (Electronics, Furniture, Software, Stationery). It helps identify top-performing regions and categories to support strategic business decisions.

**Key Insights**
1. **West is the strongest region by revenue**
